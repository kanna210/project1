
Meal Picker
a. What problem does your app solve or what purpose does it serve?
When you and your friend, partner, roommate, family etc don’t know what to eat, this app solves that problem.

b. Who is the intended user?
You and your friend, partner, roommate, family etc.

c. What actions will users be able to perform (e.g., sign up, add items, search, comment, etc.)?
You sign in and you can add and delete the meal items.
You can vote, cross out the items.
You can click random pick button and it selects 1 meal from meal ideas that have been voted by you and the other user.

Instead of meal picker, I may change this to Activities idea picker where you and your friend can pick activities you do for weekends or vacation.
